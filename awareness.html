Digital Scam Awareness

Protect Yourself from Online Scams

  • Never share your OTP with anyone.
  • Do not share your bank PIN or CVV.
  • Verify website URLs before logging in.
  • Avoid clicking unknown links received by SMS or email.
  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) where possible.
  • Do not install apps from unknown sources.
  • Report suspicious messages immediately.

Common Types of Scams

Scam Type Description
UPI Fraud Fake payment requests or QR code scams.
OTP Scam Fraudsters ask for OTPs to access accounts.
Job Scam Fake job offers requesting money or personal information.
Lottery Scam Messages claiming you've won a prize and asking for payment.
